What the display shows vs. actual speed

Dashed grey is a perfect instrument. Below 4800 RPM both traces sit on it.

The failure is silent. Past about 4900 RPM every second revolution arrives while the previous 12.5 ms burst is still running, so the latch ignores it. Exactly half the revolutions are dropped and the display settles on a steady, confident half reading — 6000 RPM shows as 3000. Nothing flickers or blanks to warn you.

Worst-case error

At low speed both are limited by the same ±1 revolution quantisation; only the behaviour at the ceiling differs.
